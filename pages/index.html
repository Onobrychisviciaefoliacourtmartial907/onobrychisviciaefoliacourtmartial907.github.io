About me

Background

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Chemistry from the State University of Campinas (UNICAMP, 2015), and a Master's degree in Organic Chemistry (2018) and a Ph.D. in Chemistry (2022) from São Paulo State University (UNESP/IQAr). During my training, I carried out two research internships at the University of Geneva (Switzerland) under the supervision of Profs. Emerson Queiroz and Jean-Luc Wolfender (2013 and 2016/2017). In 2019/2020, I was a Fulbright Visiting Scholar at the Skaggs School of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences, University of California San Diego, in the laboratory of Prof. Pieter Dorrestein, where I have been a postdoctoral researcher since 2022.

My background is in the chemistry of natural products, with a focus on the chemical, chemotaxonomic, metabolomic, and biological study of secondary metabolites from plants of the Brazilian biodiversity. My current research leverages data science approaches to reanalyze public metabolomics data and investigate the distribution of specific classes of metabolites, including bile acids, N-acyl lipids, and carnitines. This work has led to the discovery of thousands of previously uncharacterized compounds, substantially expanding the known chemical space of these classes. Many of these newly identified molecules are associated with health-related phenotypes, highlighting the need to understand their mechanistic roles in disease. I also lead the development of the Collaborative Microbial Metabolite Center knowledgebase (CMMC-kb) and contribute to community resources such as plantMASST.

My work is supported by a K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award from the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke (NINDS), through which I investigate the roles of gut microbial metabolites in HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders, and by a pilot project from the National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA) exploring microbial metabolites in the gut-brain axis during opioid exposure. My long-term goal is to lead an independent research group studying how microbial metabolites shape the microbiome-brain axis in health and disease.
